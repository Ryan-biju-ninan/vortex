Important Medical Disclaimer

This software is a clinical decision-support tool only. It screens for risk based on qSOFA criteria but does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment. This tool is designed for educational and demonstration purposes.
